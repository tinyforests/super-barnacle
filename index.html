Plants that co-evolved with this ground.

Indigenous plants have grown alongside local soils, climate and wildlife for thousands of years. Development and introduced species have displaced most of them.

Planting what belongs restores local biodiversity, feeds local wildlife, and builds climate resilience — starting in your own garden.

At Gardener & Son, we acknowledge the Wurundjeri Woi Wurrung people of the Kulin Nation, the Traditional Custodians of the land on which we live and work. We honour their enduring connection to Country — and the grasslands, woodlands and wetlands that once flourished here through generations of care and balance. These living systems were not lost to time, but to disconnection. Our work seeks to rekindle that relationship, weaving biodiversity back into the urban fabric.
