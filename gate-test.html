Box Ironbark Forest

EVC 61 · Vulnerable · Goldfields

Open forest of Red Ironbark and Grey Box on shallow gravelly soils, with a sparse shrub layer and a ground flora of lilies, everlastings and wallaby-grasses. A winter-flowering nectar engine for honeyeaters and the endangered Swift Parrot.

  • Canopy Eucalyptus tricarpa — Red Ironbark
  • Mid-storey Acacia pycnantha — Golden Wattle
  • Ground Chrysocephalum apiculatum — Common Everlasting
